# Paddy pilot field test

Run the first farmer sessions in Krishna and West Godavari. Recruit speakers of the languages they actually use and ask a local Krishi Vigyan Kendra or extension reviewer to check the paddy wording and source applicability. Give reviewers the cited PDF pages, the issue and validity dates, and the answer in each language.

## Farmer session

Let each participant start on their own phone if possible. Ask them to think aloud while you observe. Record where they hesitate before offering help, then ask them to explain the answer in their own words. Use a participant code in the notes.

| Task | Observe |
| --- | --- |
| Select the district and paddy. Ask whether irrigation is needed on a date that has reviewed evidence. | Can the participant find the district and tell whether weather and crop guidance are available? |
| Leave the crop stage unknown. Ask “Should I irrigate my paddy on 2026-11-24 if rain is expected?” | Can the participant locate the follow-up, choose the stage, and keep the original question in view? |
| Choose a date with no applicable guidance or try the same crop outside its reviewed area. | Can the participant explain what evidence is missing and whether they should act on the result? |
| Open the source panel and give feedback. | Can the participant find the supporting source, date, page or section, and a feedback option? |

For the archived replay, use a drying-soil observation and choose the **Milk to grain hardening** stage when asked. The original Krishna decision should remain the same across languages, with a 1 mm district forecast, the 24 November date, and the same two passages. When using the replay with farmers, clearly identify it as a historical exercise.

Suggested equivalent prompts

- English — “Should I irrigate my paddy on 2026-11-24 if rain is expected?”
- Telugu — “వర్షం పడే అవకాశం ఉంటే 2026-11-24 తేదీన నా వరి పొలానికి నీరు పెట్టాలా?”
- Hindi — “बारिश की संभावना हो तो क्या 2026-11-24 को धान की सिंचाई करूँ?”

Record the device, language, task completion without help, time to first answer, any mistaken interpretation of the recommended action, and the issue to address. Ask the reviewer to compare scope, action, quantities, uncertainty, and dates against the cited passages. Mark each item as reviewed, needs revision, or awaiting review and record the reviewer and date.

## Pilot status

On 23 November 2026, headless Chrome at a 360-pixel viewport passed the coverage display, crop-stage follow-up and keyboard focus, retained question, cited answer, and feedback submission in English, Telugu, and Hindi. The protected admin page showed all three feedback records and the maintenance queue. The checks used an isolated Docker database.

External farmer sessions and independent language and agronomic reviews are awaiting participants and a reviewer.
